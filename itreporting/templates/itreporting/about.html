Our Mission

Sheffield Hallam University is committed to providing exceptional education and support to students from all backgrounds. Our Module Registration System is designed to make the student experience as smooth and efficient as possible.

We believe in empowering students to take control of their academic journey by providing them with intuitive tools to browse, select, and register for modules that align with their career goals and personal interests.

Our Vision: To be a leading institution in student-centered technology and innovative learning solutions.

Our History

Sheffield Hallam University has a rich history dating back to 1843, when the Sheffield School of Design was founded. Over the years, we have evolved to become one of the UK's leading modern universities.

Key Milestones

  • 1843: Founded as Sheffield School of Design
  • 1969: Became Sheffield Polytechnic
  • 1992: Granted university status as Sheffield Hallam University
  • 2020: Launched innovative digital module registration system
  • 2025: Serving over 30,000 students across diverse disciplines
